# draft-outreach

## Goal

Get the recipient to open the resume or GitHub link.

The message is not a second resume and not a product manifesto.

## Inputs

- recipient / channel;
- exact role;
- company research;
- fit report;
- selected resume version;
- project links when available.

## Structure

Recommended order:

1. who the candidate is + role + availability;
2. one memorable thesis relevant to this team;
3. 2–3 evidence points;
4. one company-specific observation or question;
5. links / attachment note.

## Candidate-side / recruiter-side mirror

For recruiter Agent companies, a strong angle may be:

> I realized my own job-search workflow is the candidate-side mirror of your recruiter Agent: job discovery, evidence-backed ranking, outreach, pipeline state, and preference learning. I extracted it into reusable Agent Skills and published the workflow as an inspectable artifact.

Use only when the repository actually exists.

## Tone

- concise;
- specific;
- plain text friendly;
- no exaggerated praise;
- no "passionate about AI" filler unless it adds information;
- no claim that the company is a perfect fit.

## Human approval boundary

Drafting is safe to do autonomously. Sending an email, submitting a form, or messaging a recruiter requires explicit approval at the point of action.
