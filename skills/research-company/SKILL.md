# research-company

## Goal

Answer one practical question:

> If the candidate joins this company, what real system/product will they work on, and is that layer durable enough to be worth the career bet?

This is not generic company background research.

## Research dimensions

### Product reality

Identify:

- actual shipped products;
- primary users / customers;
- what job the product replaces or improves;
- whether the product has repeated usage or only demo value;
- whether the product is consumer, developer, enterprise, infrastructure, or research.

Separate company products from community projects and marketing demos.

### Technical layer

Map the company to one or more layers:

- foundation model;
- post-training / evaluation;
- routing / inference;
- Agent runtime / harness;
- memory / context / policy;
- skills / plugin distribution;
- workflow product;
- vertical/domain application;
- developer platform;
- UI / human-Agent interaction.

Then ask:

> If frontier models and native harnesses become 30% stronger tomorrow, does this company's value increase, remain, or collapse?

Thin wrappers that are trivially absorbed by stronger native harnesses should receive a durability penalty.

### Recent innovation

Prefer the latest 6–12 months. Look for:

- new model releases;
- runtime/harness changes;
- evaluation systems;
- agentic products;
- distribution/ecosystem changes;
- new business model or wedge;
- important open-source releases.

Distinguish official claims from inference.

### Business / operating reality

Check when available:

- funding stage and recency;
- team size;
- location / remote policy;
- target customer;
- pricing / monetization;
- whether the role is active;
- signs of hiring expansion or contraction;
- reputational / organizational risks relevant to the candidate.

Do not convert rumor into fact. Label community claims as community claims.

## Output

Produce a concise dossier:

```text
Company thesis
What they actually sell
Technical/product layer
Recent innovation
Durable moat candidates
Main weaknesses / risks
Why this specific role matters
What the candidate should challenge in interview
Sources / evidence quality
```

## Non-goal

Do not rewrite a resume here. Research should feed `evaluate-fit` and `tailor-resume`.
